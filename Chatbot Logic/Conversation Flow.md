Chatbot intergrates "Order Status" with "Return & Refunds" based on correspondence between option. "Order Status" and "Return & Refunds" must corrrespond.

"Order Status" comes before "Return & Refunds" 

"Order Status" has several options. These options are "Order Available", "Order Handpicked", "Order Placed in Chart", "Order Paid and Checkout", "Order Cleared For Shipping", "Order Shipped" "Order on its Way", "Order Available For Picking" "Order Picked by Customer"

"Return & Refunds" matches/correspondence with the "Order Status" options only with options that comes after "Order Handpicked". 

Chatbot says, "Hello, how do I help you today?" and first gives the two main options, that is, "Order Status" and "Return & Refunds" where the customer can choose one and then each option brings the options under it.

Choosing "Return & Refunds" the appropriate "Order Status" are shown. 

For unavailable options, chatbot gives advice like a customer care agent does or a friend would do. 

For unsupported requests, chatbot says "I am not in the position to help with this kindly ask for another request" and advices the customer to escalate the issue with humans.
